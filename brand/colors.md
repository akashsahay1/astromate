# Astromate brand

| Share | Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60% | Astromate Yellow | `#FEBC0C` | Logo, dominant brand surfaces (nav bar, highlights) |
| 30% | Astromate Red | `#DB0007` | Buttons, CTAs, accents, app icon background |
| 10% | White / Black / Astromate Indigo | `#FFFFFF` / `#000000` / `#312E81` | Text, backgrounds, astrologer-side accents |

- `logo/` — "A" mark (`logo_mark*`) and wordmark (`logo_wordmark*`), transparent PNGs.
- `app-icon/` — 1024px icon (yellow A on red), Android adaptive foreground + monochrome.
- `favicons/` — favicon.ico (16–64), PNG sizes, apple-touch-icon (180). For the website: copy into its `public/`.

Source art: client logo shared 2026-10-31 (mark) and website `logo-text.png` (wordmark), recoloured.
